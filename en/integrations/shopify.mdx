## How to connect Shopify with your Doko account?

Follow the steps below to link your Shopify store with your chatbot. The process is quick and only requires authorizing access from your Shopify account.

**Location:** Dashboard → Settings → Integrations → Shopify

## Manage Shopify knowledge base

- ### Store prompt
This prompt provides store-specific context (products, collections, policies, tone) so the chatbot can respond accurately and maintain brand identity.

- ### Product synchronization
Allows your chatbot to know your store's products so that when users ask about your products, it can respond directly and automatically.

- ### Search collections
Collections organize your products and make it easier for customers to quickly find what they're looking for, improving navigation, shopping experience, and your conversions.

## Real difference between good and bad inventory

<CardGroup cols={1}>
  <Card title="Bad inventory" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    - Incomplete products or with confusing information
    - Mixed or poorly configured variants
    - Incorrect stock (products that "exist", but don't exist)
    - Disorganized navigation, difficult to explore
  </Card>
  <Card title="Good inventory" icon="check-circle">
    - Complete products: clear photos, accurate descriptions, correct prices
    - Well-organized variants (sizes, colors, models)

  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to uninstall the Shopify integration

If you need to uninstall the Shopify integration, you can do it from two places:

### From Doko

1. Go to **Dashboard → Settings → Integrations**
2. Find the **Shopify** integration in the list
3. Click the **Disconnect** or **Uninstall** button
4. Confirm the action to complete the uninstallation

When uninstalling from Doko, the connection between your account and Shopify will be removed, but the app will remain installed in your Shopify store.

### From Shopify

1. Sign in to your Shopify admin panel
2. Go to **Settings → Apps and sales channels**
3. Find the **Doko** app in the list of installed apps
4. Click **Uninstall** or **Remove app**
5. Confirm the uninstallation

When uninstalling from Shopify, Doko's access to your store will be revoked and the integration will be completely disconnected.
