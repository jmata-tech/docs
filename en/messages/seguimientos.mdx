## What are follow-up messages?

Follow-up messages are automatic messages sent to your customers based on their current situation. They can serve as reminders, to re-engage a conversation with an interested customer, or to drive a pending action.

Other advantages of follow-ups are:

- **Recover conversations**: Re-engage customers who stopped responding
- **Automate follow-up**: Don't lose opportunities by forgetting to do manual follow-up
- **Improve experience**: Keep communication fluid and proactive with your customers
- **Increase conversions**: Improve your response rates and sales closure

<Tip>Follow-ups are essential to maximize the value of each conversation and not lose business opportunities</Tip>

<Warning>
Delay times have limitations depending on the platform:
- **Instagram and Messenger**: Maximum 24 hours after the last interaction (Meta API policy)
- **WhatsApp**: Can use more time if you use templates pre-approved by Meta
</Warning>

## Prerequisites

Before creating your first follow-up, make sure you have:

1. **Active integration**: At least one active messaging integration (WhatsApp, Instagram or Messenger)
2. **Existing conversations**: Follow-ups are applied to active conversations with your customers
3. **WhatsApp templates** (optional): If you use WhatsApp and want to send after 24 hours, you need pre-approved templates

<Warning>Without an active integration, follow-ups will not be sent</Warning>

With this description, the AI will know when to cancel all pending follow-ups for a conversation.

## Limitations and considerations

### Time limits by platform

| Platform | Maximum time without template | Maximum time with template |
|----------|-------------------------------|----------------------------|
| **Instagram** | 24 hours | Not applicable (no templates) |
| **Messenger** | 24 hours | Not applicable (no templates) |
| **WhatsApp** | 24 hours | No limit (with approved template) |

<Warning>
Meta policies limit sending messages on Instagram and Messenger to 24 hours after the user's last interaction, regardless of whether you use templates or not.
</Warning>

### WhatsApp costs

When using WhatsApp templates for follow-ups, keep in mind that:

- An additional charge will be generated for using the WhatsApp API
- Prices vary according to your plan and region
- Check updated prices in the official WhatsApp Business API documentation

<Accordion title="Frequently asked questions">

### How many follow-ups can I create?

Currently you can only have 5 active follow-ups per chatbot.

### Can I edit a follow-up after creating it?

Yes, you can edit any configuration of an existing follow-up. Changes will apply to future follow-ups.

### What happens if a customer responds before the follow-up is sent?

If a customer responds before the delay time is met, the follow-up is automatically canceled to avoid sending unnecessary messages.

### Can I use the same follow-up for different platforms?

Yes, you can configure the same follow-up to work on all your active integrations. However, keep in mind the time limitations of each platform.

### How do I know if a follow-up was sent correctly?

You can check in conversation logs if a follow-up was activated and sent. The system will show you when and to whom each follow-up was sent.

### What's the difference between letting AI generate the message and writing it manually?

When AI generates the message, it automatically adapts to the context of each specific conversation, creating more natural and personalized messages. A manual message will be the same for all customers but gives you total control over the content.

### Can I schedule follow-ups for specific times of the day?

Follow-ups are sent based on time elapsed since the last interaction, not on specific hours of the day. If you need to schedule sends at specific times, consider using scheduled campaigns.

### Do follow-ups work with all types of conversations?

Yes, follow-ups work with any type of active conversation in your configured integrations (WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger).
