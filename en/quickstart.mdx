## How to create your chatbot?
<Steps>

  <Step title="Access your dashboard">
   Click on the 'Create Chatbot' button located in the upper right corner of your dashboard.
   <Frame>
    <img src="/images/quickstart/en/dashboard.png" alt="Dashboard" />
   </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Talk to the chatbot creation assistant">
    Indicate your company name so it matches your chatbot's name.
   <Frame>
    <img src="/images/quickstart/en/nombre.png" alt="Creation assistant" />
   </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Describe your business">
    Tell the assistant about your business so it can configure your chatbot appropriately.
   <Frame>
    <img src="/images/quickstart/en/descripcion.png" alt="Describe your business" />
   </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Creating the chatbot">
    Wait just a few seconds for the assistant to create your chatbot correctly.

    When it's created, you'll be automatically redirected to your new bot.
   <Frame>
    <img src="/images/quickstart/en/creando.png" alt="Chatbot name" />
   </Frame>
  </Step>

</Steps>
